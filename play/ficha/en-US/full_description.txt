MyRideStats measures how far you lean your motorcycle in every curve. Put the phone in your pocket any way you like, tap “Start ride” and go: the phone's position doesn't matter.

ON THE RIDE
• Live lean angle, with your maximum to the left and to the right
• Time leaned to each side and the balance between them
• Keeps recording with the screen off

HISTORY
• Every ride with its map, colored by lean angle
• Every curve with its angle and its entry, minimum and exit speeds
• Lean chart over the ride and curves grouped by angle
• Animated replay of the ride

ANALYSIS
• Symmetry: do you lean the same to both sides?
• Progress by week or month
• Heat map of all your rides
• Your regular curves: detects the curves you ride again and shows how you improve on each one

SHARE
• Export your rides as GPX
• Image card with the ride's stats to share

PRIVACY
No accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your rides are stored only on your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
The app combines the gyroscope, the accelerometer and GPS speed to estimate the bike's real angle, whatever the phone's position. A few seconds stopped at the start are enough to calibrate. Nothing is measured below 15 km/h.

Light or dark theme, in English and Spanish. Maps from OpenStreetMap.

SAFETY: don't handle your phone while riding. The angles are estimates: always ride within your limits and the law.
